Service 01

Gold Jewellery Loan

Unlock funds against your personal gold jewellery—including necklaces, bangles, chains, and rings—while retaining full ownership.

  • Eligible Jewellery: 18K to 22K gold jewellery subject to purity and condition checks.
  • Valuation Process: Non-destructive XRF purity testing and calibrated net weight measurement.
  • Loan Assessment: Calculated on net gold weight excluding non-gold stones or clasps.

Service 02

Gold Ornament Loan

Tailored for traditional and heirloom gold ornaments, with careful handling and precise assessment of pure gold content.

  • Eligible Items: Heritage necklaces, temple ornaments, traditional kadas, and bridal sets.
  • Assessment Process: Respectful visual and electronic inspection to preserve delicate craftsmanship.
  • Documentation: Itemized pledge receipt recording ornament count, gross weight, and net gold weight.

Service 03 — Policy Dependent

Gold Coin Loan

Available only for eligible, hallmarked, or bank-issued gold coins within applicable regulatory weight limits and company policy.

  • Terms & Conditions: Accepted strictly subject to company policy and regulatory weight caps per borrower.
  • Purity Standards: Hallmarked or certified minted coins verified at the branch.
  • Custody: Sealed in tamper-evident packaging with detailed pledge documentation.

Service 04

Business Gold Loan

Bridge working capital, seasonal inventory, or vendor payment needs by pledging eligible gold assets, subject to lender policies.

  • Designed For: Retailers, traders, self-employed professionals, and MSMEs.
  • Transparent Assessment: Clear LTV limits, interest structure, and repayment schedules.
  • Supportive Process: Straightforward KYC and business-friendly branch assistance.
