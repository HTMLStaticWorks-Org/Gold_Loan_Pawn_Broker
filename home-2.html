Step-by-Step Appraisal

4 Steps to Value Your Gold

See how every ornament moves through four transparent checkpoints before a loan offer is finalized.

Stage 01

Weight Measurement

Gross weight is recorded on a calibrated digital balance, and non-gold stones or clasps are accounted for to find net weight.

Stage 02

Purity Verification

Electronic and XRF karat testing verifies exact gold purity without melting, scratching, or damaging your jewellery.

Stage 03

Applicable Gold Rate

Your verified purity and net weight are multiplied by the applicable daily per-gram gold benchmark rate.

Stage 04

Eligible Loan Assessment

An indicative Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio is applied to present your eligible loan amount and clear repayment terms.
