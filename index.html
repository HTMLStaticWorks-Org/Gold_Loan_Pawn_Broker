Precision Appraisal

How We Value Every Gram of Gold

Our branch appraisal desk combines calibrated digital weighing with modern XRF purity verification so you see every step clearly.

  • Gold Weight Assessment: Calibrated precision balances measure gross and net gold weight accurately.
  • Purity Verification: Non-destructive karat analysis verifies 18K, 20K, 22K, or 24K composition.
  • Applicable Gold Rate: Linked to transparent daily valuation benchmarks.
  • Loan Eligibility Assessment: Calculated within applicable LTV norms and company policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Purity is assessed using non-destructive electronic/XRF testing and visual hallmark inspection in your presence, without damaging your jewellery.
Estimated loan value is based on the net gold weight (excluding stones or non-gold parts), verified purity, the applicable daily gold rate, and permitted LTV percentage.
Eligible gold bangles, chains, necklaces, rings, and earrings are considered, subject to purity standards and company acceptance policy.
Standard identity proof, address proof, and photographs as per applicable KYC norms are typically requested. See our Eligibility & Documents page for details.
Repayment schedules, interest servicing, and principal closure options are detailed in your loan agreement prior to disbursement.
Upon full settlement of principal and applicable dues, the pledged gold is retrieved from the vault and handed back after identity verification.
