Gold Items We Evaluate for Loans

Review the jewellery and ornament categories evaluated at our branches. This is a gold-loan collateral catalogue—acceptance depends on purity verification and lender policy.

Category 01

Gold Bangles

Solid, carved, and daily-wear gold bangles are among the most commonly pledged ornaments for gold loans.

  • Purity Information: Typically 20K to 22K; verified via non-destructive XRF analysis.
  • Valuation Considerations: Hollow bangles with lac or wax filling are assessed strictly on net gold content.
  • Condition: Must pass physical and purity verification at the branch.
Category 02

Gold Chains

Classic link, box, rope, and heritage gold chains are weighed on high-precision balances for clean valuation.

  • Purity Information: Commonly 18K, 20K, or 22K depending on craftsmanship.
  • Valuation Considerations: Clasp springs or non-gold hooks (if any) are accounted for in net weight.
  • Precision Check: Measured to two decimal places in grams in your presence.
Category 03

Gold Rings

Plain gold bands, signet rings, and traditional rings are evaluated using density and electronic purity verification.

  • Purity Information: Evaluated across 18K–22K standards.
  • Valuation Considerations: Mounted stones, pearls, or enamel are excluded to arrive at net gold weight.
  • Multiple Pieces: Can be combined with other eligible ornaments in a single pledge.
Category 04

Gold Earrings

Jhumkas, studs, hoops, and drop earrings are weighed inside draft-shielded precision balances for exact gram readings.

  • Purity Information: Checked for hallmarking and consistent karat purity across both pieces.
  • Valuation Considerations: Non-gold screw backs or gemstone drops are excluded from net weight.
  • Careful Custody: Packed securely in cushioned, sealed pouches after appraisal.

How Value Is Determined

Key Gold Valuation Factors

Gold Purity

Higher karat purity (e.g., 22K vs. 18K) directly increases the per-gram valuation rate.

Net Gold Weight

Only pure gold weight is counted—stones, beads, wax, and non-gold parts are excluded.

Applicable Market Rate

Linked to transparent daily benchmark gold prices applicable on the date of pledge.

Condition & Policy

Final acceptance and LTV percentage follow lender guidelines and regulatory norms.
