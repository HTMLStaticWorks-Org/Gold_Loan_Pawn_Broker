A Clear 6-Step Gold Loan Process

Every stage from walking into our branch to gold inspection, valuation, documentation, and repayment is designed to be transparent and easy to follow.

01
Step 01

Branch Visit & Initial Consultation

Visit a branch with your gold jewellery or ornaments and standard KYC documents. A loan officer welcomes you, understands your requirement, and explains the applicable process before appraisal begins.

02
Step 02

Gold Inspection

Our trained appraiser conducts a careful visual and physical inspection of each ornament—checking hallmark stamps, joints, clasps, and identifying any non-gold stones or inlays.

Step 03

Purity Verification

Using non-destructive handheld and benchtop XRF testing, we verify exact karat purity (such as 18K, 20K, or 22K) right in front of you without damaging your jewellery.

Step 04

Weight Assessment

Your ornaments are weighed on a calibrated digital balance (e.g., 0.01g precision). Gross weight and net gold weight (excluding stones or non-gold parts) are recorded transparently.

05
Step 05

Loan Terms & Documentation

Based on net gold weight, purity, applicable daily rate, and LTV norms, you receive a clear loan offer. Interest rate, tenure, charges, and KYC verification are reviewed and documented in a signed pledge agreement.

Step 06

Approval, Disbursement & Repayment Information

Once approved, your ornaments are sealed in a tamper-evident pouch for vault custody, and funds are disbursed per your chosen mode. Upon full repayment of principal and applicable dues, your pledged gold is returned against identity verification.
