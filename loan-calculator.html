Important Disclaimer

Important Terms & Valuation Rules

This calculator provides an indicative estimate only using the formula: Estimated Gold Value = Eligible Net Gold Weight × Applicable Gold Rate, and Estimated Eligible Loan = Estimated Gold Value × Applicable LTV Percentage.

  • Final loan amounts depend on physical & XRF purity verification at the branch.
  • Weight of stones, pearls, lac, wax, or non-gold clasps is deducted from gross weight.
  • Applicable LTV limits, regulatory rules, and lender policies govern final sanction.
What Affects Your Estimate

Understanding Your Valuation Breakdown

Two ornaments with the same gross weight can have different eligible loan values depending on their verified gold purity and net gold content.

  • Gross vs. Net Weight: Stones, beads, enamel, and non-gold clasps are excluded before valuation.
  • Karat Purity Factor: 22K (91.6%) and 18K (75.0%) ornaments scale proportionally from the 24K benchmark rate.
  • LTV Ceiling: Regulatory Loan-to-Value caps and chosen loan scheme determine the final eligible percentage.
