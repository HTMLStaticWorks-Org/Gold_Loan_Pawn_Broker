Eligibility & Required Documents

Understand the general eligibility criteria, accepted gold items, and KYC documents typically requested. Final approval and requirements depend on lender policy and applicable regulations.

Applicant Criteria

Who Can Apply (General Eligibility)

Meeting basic eligibility criteria helps ensure a smooth branch appraisal. Loan approval is not guaranteed and remains subject to verification.

Age Requirement

Applicants must be adult residents (typically 18 years or above) eligible to enter a loan agreement.

Gold Ownership

Pledged gold ornaments must belong lawfully to the applicant with ownership declaration.

KYC Compliance

Valid government-issued identity and address verification is mandatory under applicable norms.

Purity & Policy

Gold items must meet minimum karat purity standards and branch acceptance guidelines.

Collateral Guidelines

Gold Items Accepted & Not Accepted

Commonly Considered

  • Gold bangles, kadas, and bracelets (18K–22K)
  • Gold chains, necklaces, and harams
  • Gold rings, earrings, studs, and jhumkas
  • Eligible hallmarked gold coins (strictly per policy & weight cap)

Generally Not Considered

  • Gold-plated, rolled-gold, or imitation jewellery
  • Raw gold dust, unrefined nuggets, or primary bullion bars
  • Items whose ownership cannot be declared or verified
  • Ornaments below minimum acceptable purity thresholds
KYC Checklist

Commonly Requested Documents

Exact documentation depends on loan amount, borrower profile, and prevailing regulatory requirements.

Identity Proof

Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving Licence (as applicable).

Address Proof

Current residential address proof such as Aadhaar, Passport, or recent utility bill.

Photographs

Recent passport-size colour photographs for loan application and pledge records.

Bank Details

Active bank account details for electronic disbursement and repayment reference.

Dual Verification

KYC & Gold Verification Process

At the branch, both your identity documents and your gold ornaments undergo transparent verification before any pledge is finalized.

  • Original KYC documents are verified against standard guidelines.
  • XRF and physical tests confirm karat purity and elemental composition.
  • Net gold weight and ornament photographs are recorded on the pledge receipt.
